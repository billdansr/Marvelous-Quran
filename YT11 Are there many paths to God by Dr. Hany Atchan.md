# Are there many paths to God?

Understanding  **لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ** requires of me that I refuse to place myself in a position of judgement over anybody else. It implies requires of me that I do not attach my faith and my belief and my relationship with **الله سبحانه وتعالى** to the behavior or the decisions of any other human being. Our relationship with **الله سبحانه وتعالى** is purely one on one and it doesn't have to go through any intermediaries.

Neither I can judge you, nor you should judge me. I may have reach different conclusions than yours and you may have reach different conclusions than mine and that's fine for me in my relationship with **الله سبحانه وتعالى**. Allah knows what's in your heart regardless of what you label or what other people label yourself. Allah is The Creator and **The Sustainer** of all his creation. 

> [!tip]+ Al-Kahf (18:29)
> Say: Truth is from your lord! Therefore, whoever wants to, let him believe, and whoever wants to, let him deny.
> وَقُلِ الْحَقُّ مِنْ رَبِّكُمْ ۖ **فَمَنْ شَاءَ فَلْيُؤْمِنْ وَمَنْ شَاءَ فَلْيَكْفُرْ** ۚ إِنَّا أَعْتَدْنَا لِلظَّالِمِينَ نَارًا أَحَاطَ بِهِمْ سُرَادِقُهَا ۚ وَإِنْ يَسْتَغِيثُوا يُغَاثُوا بِمَاءٍ كَالْمُهْلِ يَشْوِي الْوُجُوهَ ۚ بِئْسَ الشَّرَابُ وَسَاءَتْ مُرْتَفَقًا

> [!tip]+ Al-Baqara (2:256)
> Let there be no compulsion in matters of religion. Indeed the path to guidance is distinguished from the path to misguidance.
> **لَا إِكْرَاهَ فِي الدِّينِ ۖ قَدْ تَبَيَّنَ الرُّشْدُ مِنَ الْغَيِّ** ۚ فَمَنْ يَكْفُرْ بِالطَّاغُوتِ وَيُؤْمِنْ بِاللَّهِ فَقَدِ اسْتَمْسَكَ بِالْعُرْوَةِ الْوُثْقَىٰ لَا انْفِصَامَ لَهَا ۗ وَاللَّهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ
>

> [!tip]+ Al-Maaida (5:105)
> O you who believe! Worry about yourselves! It does not affect you if someone goes astray as long as you seek guidance! To Allahh is your ultimate return—all of you—and He shall tell you all that you were doing.
> يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا عَلَيْكُمْ أَنْفُسَكُمْ ۖ لَا يَضُرُّكُمْ مَنْ ضَلَّ إِذَا اهْتَدَيْتُمْ ۚ إِلَى اللَّهِ مَرْجِعُكُمْ جَمِيعًا فَيُنَبِّئُكُمْ بِمَا كُنْتُمْ تَعْمَلُونَ
>

> [!CONCLUSION] 
> My faith and my certainty do not depend on others, do not depend on whether or not I see a certain person becoming a muslim or not becoming a muslim. In order for me to have a pure relationship with **الله** alone, I should get rid of all the phobias and all the expectations and all the fears and all the convictions that I've inherited from others. Therefore, I should move from a belief based on following others to a belief based on my own certainty and my own relationship with **الله**.
