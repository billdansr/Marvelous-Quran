# Can You Be Successful but Unhappy?

> [!QUESTION]
> How do you define success?

Happiness determines the success in the world.

Material wealth, possession, and pleasures in general do not bring happiness.

Societies that have truly mastered the abilities to accumulate wealth and to live in a very comfortable luxurious way, i.e. wealthy societies such as in the west, these are the societies where people commit the most suicide, where people have access and actually abuse drugs more often than in any other society, they abuse alcohol, they waste their time, and they have a famous saying of "killing time"—doing some activities to kill time and the ultimate killing of time is suicide.

This issue won't be able to be resolved unless we first understand what does **الله سبحانه وتعالى** guide us as to the definition of success in this life.

> [!QUOTE] Al-Kahf 18:103-104
> قُلْ هَلْ نُنَبِّئُكُم بِٱلْأَخْسَرِينَ أَعْمَـٰلًا ١٠٣
> ٱلَّذِينَ ضَلَّ سَعْيُهُمْ فِى ٱلْحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنْيَا وَهُمْ يَحْسَبُونَ أَنَّهُمْ يُحْسِنُونَ صُنْعًا ١٠٤
> 
> Say: Shall we tell you about those who are the worst losers? They are the ones whose deeds in the life are misguided, all the while thinking that they are doing good things.

Allah **الله** defines the worldly pleasures using a term called zina[^1] and it's like someone traveling a long distance and then he decides to stop for a couple of days to resupply his provisions or to resupply what he needs in terms of water or clothing or whatever basic needs he needs for the rest of his journey and then he gets distracted and starts collecting and gathering decorations.

The Quran does not use the word happiness per se to define the state of pleasure in this life, instead it uses the word satisfaction which is rida[^2].

> [!QUOTE] Ad-Duhaa 93:4-5
> مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ ٣
> وَلَلْـَٔاخِرَةُ خَيْرٌۭ لَّكَ مِنَ ٱلْأُولَىٰ ٤
> 
> Indeed, the later life is best for you, and your lord shall give you to ensure that you are joyful.

Make sure that you own your possessions and not your possessions that owns you. Make sure that you control your drive to accumulate wealth and not the other way around. Make sure your worldly pleasures and your worldly goals do not become addictions. By creating addictions for yourself, you become enslaved, a subservient person to those needs instead of the other way around.

> [!QUOTE] Al-Ahzab 33:4
> **مَّا جَعَلَ ٱللَّهُ لِرَجُلٍۢ مِّن قَلْبَيْنِ فِى جَوْفِهِ**ۦ ۚ وَمَا جَعَلَ أَزْوَٰجَكُمُ ٱلَّـٰٓـِٔى تُظَـٰهِرُونَ مِنْهُنَّ أُمَّهَـٰتِكُمْ ۚ وَمَا جَعَلَ أَدْعِيَآءَكُمْ أَبْنَآءَكُمْ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمْ قَوْلُكُم بِأَفْوَٰهِكُمْ ۖ وَٱللَّهُ يَقُولُ ٱلْحَقَّ وَهُوَ يَهْدِى ٱلسَّبِيلَ ٤
> 
> Allah did not allow a person to have two hearts inside him.

[^1]: Zina is decoration.

[^2]: Rida is joy. It is a little bit more than joy, joy at the level of satisfaction that can only be permanent inside.
