# What is Your Personal Mission Statement?

> [!tip]+ Al-An'aam (6:162)
> Say: "My prayers and my acts of devotion, and my life, and my death are dedicated to Allahh, the lord of the worlds."
> قُلْ إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحْيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ
>

> [!QUESTION] What does it mean to dedicate my death?
> It means you live your life including all the activities where you may actually die. Those activities should be dedicated to **الله سبحانه وتعالى**.

# Exercise to Have a Mission Statement

- If you wake up tomorrow and someone gives you enough money to live so comfortably and so wealthy and so unworried about any concerns, neither for you nor for your family, children, grandchildren forever. 
- You don't have to work a minute of your life. You don't have to toil to generate your income during the rest of your whole life.
- What would you do with your time?
- A person who lives according to a personal mission statement is never bored because he has a mission in life. That person doesn't have free time that he doesn't know what to do with. That person is never confused. He knows how to spend every minute of his life.
- That person can never be used by other people, cannot be enslaved by other people, cannot be driven by other people to do their missions, cannot live in servitude to other people because he's free, he's focused, he knows exactly what his life is about.
